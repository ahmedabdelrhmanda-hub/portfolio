03. Projects

MTA Ridership Analysis

Excel · PivotTables · Dashboard

Analyzed 4 years of New York MTA daily ridership data (2020–2024) to study post-pandemic recovery across subways, buses and commuter rails. Built an interactive Excel dashboard with PivotTables and charts that showed daily subway ridership recovering from 1.2M (2020) to 3.2M (2024) — about 71% of pre-pandemic levels — with buses lagging at 60%, and identified the mid-week peak (Wednesday ≈ 3.9M riders) vs. weekends.
